OUR APPROACH

From Idea to Submission, We Follow a Clear Process.

We don't just jump into development. Every project goes through a structured process designed to turn your idea into a complete, presentable and properly documented project.

01

R

We understand the problem, investigate existing solutions, gather relevant information and establish a strong foundation for the project.

02

03

FOUNDER & LEAD

GODFRED MARCUS ANTWI

ProjectBridge was created with a simple belief: students should have access to the right technical support, guidance and expertise when turning their ideas into real projects.

As a Skills Instructor at The Rock Impact Network , I work with students and young people to develop practical technology skills and turn knowledge into something they can actually build and use.

As an Event Speaker, I've also had the opportunity to share knowledge, inspire young people and contribute to technology and educational events.

My experience across technology, design, training and project development led to the creation of ProjectBridge — a platform built to help students move from an idea to a project they can confidently submit and present.
